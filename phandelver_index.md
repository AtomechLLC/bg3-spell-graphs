## The homogeneity angle

Nearly half the Starter Set's spell list sits in reskin families — a new player's
very first exposure to D&D magic already contains the Cure family (4 of its 5
members!), the full blast template trio, and three of the damage-cantrip engine's
variants. The starter box teaches the reskin grammar from level 1.
