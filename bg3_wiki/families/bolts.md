**Shared skeleton.** WoW's bolt engine — the family that started this whole study — found in BG3's leveled single-target damage. Chromatic Orb is the element menu (a six-way [[containers|container]]), Guiding Bolt sells advantage-on-next-hit, Witch Bolt a re-zap channel, Scorching Ray splits into three rolls, Melf's Acid Arrow a lingering tick, Ice Knife a shrapnel burst, Magic Missile the auto-hit, Ray of Sickness the poison rider. The save-based wing runs the same job at melee and long range: Blight, Harm, Inflict Wounds, Disintegrate's overkill clause — and Vampiric Touch is the bolt that pays you back, BG3's one entry on WoW's whole siphon shelf.

**What varies.** Element, dice, delivery (attack roll, save, auto-hit, touch), and the rider (advantage, repeat damage, lingering tick, poison, self-heal, disintegration).

**Design read.** Single-target projectile damage is the most homogeneous shelf in every game studied — BG3's version was hiding in plain sight among the unfamilied.
