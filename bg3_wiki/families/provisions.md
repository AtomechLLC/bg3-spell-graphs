**Shared skeleton.** WoW's conjured food-and-water counter, BG3 edition: Goodberry mints camp supplies four at a time, Heroes' Feast caters the whole party (46–58 supplies plus the buff), Create or Destroy Water stocks the bar. In both games the same design job — turn a spell slot into rest-economy resources.

**What varies.** The resource minted, the batch size, and the attached buff.

**Design read.** Rest economy is a real spell-design target in both games; BG3's version pays out in camp supplies instead of conjured bread.
