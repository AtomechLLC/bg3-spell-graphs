**Shared skeleton.** One die of typed damage, scaling at character levels 5 and 10; Acid Splash vs. Poison Spray measures 0.95 on mechanics. Bone Chill is Chill Touch renamed. Three more from the unfamilied list run the same table: Toll the Dead (the d8-that-upgrades-to-d12), Produce Flame (the fire attack that doubles as a torch), and Vicious Mockery (a d4 plus a disadvantage rider). Booming Blade rides a weapon swing instead of a spell die, so it files with the [[families/smites|Smite Armoury]].

**What varies.** The die (d4–d12), delivery (attack roll vs. save), range, and the rider — slow, no-healing, pull, push, ignite, mock.

**Design read.** The same costed-variation table as tabletop, plus BG3's own twist: many of these also interact with the [[families/surfaces|surface engine]] (Fire Bolt ignites grease, Ray of Frost freezes water).
