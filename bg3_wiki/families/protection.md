**Shared skeleton.** WoW's protection rack — armors, wards, absorbs, panic buttons — reassembled from BG3's unfamilied shelf. The miss-makers (Blur's disadvantage, Mirror Image's decoys), the halvers (Blade Ward, Stoneskin, Protection from Energy), the survival clauses (Death Ward's cheat-death, Warding Bond's damage-split), the retaliators (Fire Shield, Hellish Rebuke's reaction bite — WoW's Retaliation shelf), and the panic buttons (Globe of Invulnerability, Resilient Sphere, Sanctuary). The AC-stackers stay in the [[families/ac-wardrobe|Armour-Class Wardrobe]] — different mechanism, same aisle.

**What varies.** The mechanism (disadvantage, decoy, resistance, death-trigger, immunity bubble), who it protects, and concentration cost.

**Design read.** Every game studied sells survival in the same five mechanisms; BG3 stocked all five and never grouped them.
