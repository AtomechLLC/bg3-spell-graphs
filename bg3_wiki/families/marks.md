**Shared skeleton.** WoW's Debuff Bureau, found operating in BG3 without a sign on the door. Hex and Hunter's Mark are the famous twins — bonus-action mark, +1d6 per hit, re-markable on kill; players and modders treat them as one spell with two class stamps. Bestow Curse is the menu version (a [[containers|container]] of curse riders), Contagion the disease shelf, Ray of Enfeeblement the damage-halver, Faerie Fire the advantage-vending machine, Heat Metal the sustained pay-per-proc model, and Phantasmal Force the tag that bills the target's own imagination every turn — BG3's closest thing to a warlock dot.

**What varies.** The tag's payload (damage rider, disadvantage, halved output, advantage to attackers), delivery, and concentration cost.

**Design read.** A gap-fill family from the WoW lens: single-target degradation is a product line in both games — BG3 just never shelved it together.
