**Shared skeleton.** Summon a persistent ally that fights and acts under your command: a creature chosen from a menu (Conjure Elemental/Minor Elemental, 0.94 signature similarity), a familiar, a raised corpse, a planar servant — or an *object* on the same contract: Spiritual Weapon and Flaming Sphere are summons whose 'creature' is a floating weapon or rolling fireball with its own place in your action economy.

**What varies.** The summoned thing (elemental, familiar, undead, celestial, weapon, sphere), its control verbs, and duration. Five of the seven are [[containers|containers]], their menus implemented as child spells.

**Design read.** One summoning engine spanning units, creatures, and conjured objects — the whole conjuration aisle runs on a single contract, and Larian's container system makes each menu literal.
