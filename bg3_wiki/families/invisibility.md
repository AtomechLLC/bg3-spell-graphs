**Shared skeleton.** Apply the Invisible condition to a touched creature; 0.93 on mechanics. Pass Without Trace is the concealment aura rung — +10 Stealth for the whole party, WoW's concealment shelf sold as an area buff.

**What varies.** Greater removes the breaks-on-attack rule and costs two more levels; Pass Without Trace trades true invisibility for group coverage.

**Design read.** An upgrade defined by deleting a sentence, exactly as in the SRD.
