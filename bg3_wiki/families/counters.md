**Shared skeleton.** WoW's interrupt-and-purge desk, BG3 edition — each spell exists to negate somebody else's. Counterspell is the reaction interrupt; Silence the standing area lockout (a zone that pre-interrupts every verbal cast inside it — WoW files its silences with the interrupts, and so does this shelf); Calm Emotions cancels charm and fear across the party; Daylight burns away magical darkness; See Invisibility revokes the invisible condition's benefits. The status-scrubbing cousins live in the [[families/cleanse|Cleanse Counter]] — that desk removes what already landed; this one stops it working at all.

**What varies.** The target of the negation (a spell mid-cast, a caster's voice, a mental status, a darkness spell, a stealth state) and the delivery (reaction, zone, burst).

**Design read.** Negation is a product line in both games: WoW sells six interrupts on six classes; BG3 sells five cancels with five different nouns.
