**Shared skeleton.** Draw a wall shape on the ground; it deals typed damage to creatures inside or crossing; signature similarity runs 0.86–0.97 across all five — the tightest template family in the game.

**What varies.** The material (fire/ice/stone/thorns/spinning blades), damage dice, and what the wall does when touched vs. entered.

**Design read.** Larian implements every wall through one zone system, so the five spells differ mainly by particle effect and damage row.
