**Shared skeleton.** The WoW weapon-enchant rack, found as a gap in BG3's unfamilied list: spells whose only job is making weapon hits larger. The enchants proper (Elemental Weapon's five-type [[containers|container]], Magic Weapon, Shillelagh's cantrip-tier club polish) buff the object; Divine Favour buffs the wielder; Crusader's Mantle radiates the buff to the whole party; Flame Blade and Shadow Blade skip the enchanting and conjure the enhanced weapon outright. The on-hit cousins stay in the [[families/smites|Smite Armoury]] — same purpose, different engine.

**What varies.** The delivery (object enchant, wielder blessing, aura, conjured blade) and the element.

**Design read.** A gap-fill family from the WoW lens: BG3 shipped a weapon-enchant product line without ever naming it.
