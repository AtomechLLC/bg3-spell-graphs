**Shared skeleton.** Pick an area; the game paints a surface or cloud that applies a condition to anyone inside. Signature similarities run 0.88–0.96 across the family (Grease/Web 0.90, Entangle/Stinking Cloud 0.94).

**What varies.** The surface type (grease, webs, vines, fog, poison, tentacles…), its condition, and whether it's flammable, freezable, or dispellable by wind.

**Design read.** BG3's signature homogenizer. Larian rebuilt tabletop's varied area-control spells on one ground-surface system — the game's most distinctive engine, and its strongest flattening force.
