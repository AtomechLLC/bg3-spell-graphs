**Shared skeleton.** Area, save, XdY typed damage, half on success. Fireball vs. Lightning Bolt: 0.90 signature similarity. Lightning Bolt vs. Thunderwave: 0.96 — in BG3 they're nearly the same entry with different shapes. The second shelf joined from the unfamilied list: Burning Hands (the level-1 cone), Shatter (the thunder sphere), Chain Lightning (the same detonation delivered as a bounce — the WoW ruling that bouncing spells file under their damage category), Destructive Wave (the paladin's nova), and Otiluke's Freezing Sphere (the cold Fireball). Arms of Hadar is the warlock's point-blank nova (with a no-reactions rider), and Conjure Barrage the ranger's weapon-flavored cone — same detonation, ammunition as the element.

**What varies.** Shape (sphere/line/cone/cylinder/bounce), element, dice, and the save ability.

**Design read.** The tabletop template, tightened: BG3's targeting system reduces 'shape' to a data field, pulling the family even closer together than in print.
