**Shared skeleton.** Forced movement as its own product — BG3's chasm-adjacent economy. Telekinesis throws a creature (landing PRONE in the effect rows), Gust of Wind shoves the lane and stamps `OFF_BALANCED`, Grasping Vine is a summoned winch that drags enemies in. None of them deal meaningful damage on paper; all of them deal enormous damage in play, because BG3's levels are built over ledges.

**What varies.** The vector (away, toward, upward-and-down-hard), the shape (single target vs. lane), and what the movement is priced in.

**Design read.** A family invisible to tooltip text and obvious in player usage — the damage number is the fall, not the spell.
