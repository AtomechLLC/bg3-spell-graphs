**Shared skeleton.** Persistent concentration damage-zones — WoW's Blizzard/Consecration/Rain of Fire aisle, BG3 edition. Cloud of Daggers is the static no-save blender, Moonbeam the movable pillar, Spirit Guardians the zone that wears *you* as its anchor, Call Lightning the re-strikeable storm, Hunger of Hadar the darkness that bites, Insect Plague the big sphere. Distinct from the [[families/blast|Elemental Blast Template]] (one detonation) and the [[families/surfaces|Surface Engine]] (ground state, no concentration).

**What varies.** Shape, element, tick trigger (start-of-turn vs. enter vs. re-cast), and mobility (static, movable, follows caster).

**Design read.** Three area-control families now sit side by side — blasts detonate, surfaces persist, zones tick under concentration. BG3 runs all three engines at once.
