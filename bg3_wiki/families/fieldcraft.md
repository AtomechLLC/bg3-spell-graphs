**Shared skeleton.** The abilities whose job is the world, not the fight — the WoW fieldcraft bucket, BG3 edition. The interrogations (Speak with Animals / Speak with Dead / Detect Thoughts — unlock a dialogue channel that doesn't normally exist), the social tools (Friends, Disguise Self's 32-costume [[containers|container]], Feign Death), and the dungeon key (Knock). Darkvision stays with the [[families/touch-buffs|Touch-Buff Skeleton]] — its mechanism family.

**What varies.** The problem solved and the class stamp.

**Design read.** Downtime is a design space in every game studied — and BG3's version is dialogue-heavy because dialogue is BG3's world.
