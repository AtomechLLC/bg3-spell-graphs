**Shared skeleton.** The abilities whose job is the world, not the fight — the WoW fieldcraft bucket, BG3 edition. The interrogations (Speak with Animals / Speak with Dead / Detect Thoughts — unlock a dialogue channel that doesn't normally exist), the social tools (Friends, Thaumaturgy, Enhance Ability's check-advantage [[containers|container]], Disguise Self's 32-costume container, Seeming's party-wide version, Feign Death), the dungeon keys (Knock and its antonym Arcane Lock), the remote hands (Mage Hand, Minor Illusion's distraction), and the torch shelf (Light, Dancing Lights). Darkvision stays with the [[families/touch-buffs|Touch-Buff Skeleton]] — its mechanism family.

**What varies.** The problem solved and the class stamp.

**Design read.** Downtime is a design space in every game studied — and BG3's version is dialogue-heavy because dialogue is BG3's world.
