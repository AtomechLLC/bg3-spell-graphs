**Shared skeleton.** The WoW lens applied to BG3's unfamilied controls: spells whose shared purpose — deny the target its turns — had no family until the cross-game comparison exposed the gap. The panics (Fear / Confusion / Hypnotic Pattern / Dissonant Whispers' damage-plus-flee / Phantasmal Killer's fright-that-ticks), the humiliations (Hideous Laughter / Irresistible Dance / Command's five verbs / Eyebite's three-mode menu — both [[containers|containers]]), the turncoat (Crown of Madness), the attention tax (Enthrall), the transformations (Polymorph's literal `POLYMORPH_SHEEP` status — WoW's signature CC, verbatim — and Flesh to Stone's petrify ramp), the enslavement contract (Planar Binding — WoW's Enslave Demon in celestial packaging), the senses (Blindness), the exile (Banishment), and Slow as the outpatient wing. The tighter clone pairs ([[families/hold|Hold]], [[families/dominate|Dominate]], [[families/charm|Charm]], the [[families/hp-pool|sleep pool]]) keep their own families.

**What varies.** The status applied, the save, and the delivery.

**Design read.** A gap-fill family, not a merge: BG3's native pairs stay granular; this catches the controls that had nowhere to live.
