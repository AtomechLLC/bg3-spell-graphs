**Shared skeleton.** 'Shows the location of all nearby X on the minimap.' One sentence, one noun slot. The Hunter gets eight copies; the Paladin and Warlock each get one with 'Sense' stamped on the label (masked similarity ≥ 0.9 across all ten).

**What varies.** The tracked creature type. Nothing else.

**Design read.** The purest reskin family in classic WoW — a radio with eight stations sold as eight spells, plus two single-station radios sold to other classes.
