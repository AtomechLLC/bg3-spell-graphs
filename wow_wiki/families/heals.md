**Shared skeleton.** 'Heal your target for X.' Four healing classes, nine spells, one design: Flash Heal / Flash of Light / Lesser Healing Wave are the fast-expensive skin, Greater Heal / Holy Light / Healing Wave the slow-efficient skin, all measuring 0.85+ against their cross-class twins.

**What varies.** The class stamp, cast time, and coefficient — plus the Priest's vertical Lesser→Heal→Greater ladder sold as three separate spells *before* ranks multiply them.

**Design read.** The definitive cross-class clone: every healer bought the same spell in a different box. Homogeneity in classic WoW runs across classes more than within them.
