**Shared skeleton.** Movement as a product line: short-range teleports (Blink), hearth-style returns (Astral Recall, Teleport: Moonglade), sprint buffs (Sprint→Dash, another verbatim rogue→cat photocopy on `Mod Increase Speed`), travel modes (Ghost Wolf), water and air utilities (Water Walking / Levitate / Slow Fall), and fall protection (Safe Fall→Feline Grace, one aura in two books).

**What varies.** The medium (ground, water, air), the delivery, and the class stamp.

**Design read.** The [[families/teleports|Hearth Network]] handles the cities; this family covers every other way classic lets you move — or land.
