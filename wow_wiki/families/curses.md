**Shared skeleton.** Weakening without disabling, filed by function: the Warlock's modal curse rack ('Curses the target with X' — Elements/Shadow are the same spell with the school swapped, 0.85), the Priest racials that photocopy it (Hex of Weakness / Touch of Weakness are Curse of Weakness in a cassock), the marks that make targets easier to kill (Faerie Fire ×2 / Hunter's Mark), the armor shredders (Expose Armor / Sunder Armor — rogue and warrior versions of one `Mod Resistance` debuff), and the war-cries that shrink attack power (Demoralizing Shout→Roar, another warrior→bear photocopy).

**What varies.** The drained quantity (stats, armor, attack power, castability), the stacking rule, and the class stamp.

**Design read.** The [[families/status-boosts|status-boost rack]] with the sign flipped — and just as cross-class.
