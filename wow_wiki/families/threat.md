**Shared skeleton.** Every entry edits the threat table and nothing else: the taunts (Taunt / Growl / Mocking Blow / Challenging Shout / Challenging Roar — `Taunt` and `Attack Me` effects, with Taunt→Growl a verbatim cross-class pair) force attention; the dumps (Cower / Feint / Fade / Disengage / Distracting Shot — `Mod Threat` with a negative sign) shed it; Righteous Fury multiplies it passively.

**What varies.** The sign (+/−), the delivery, and the class stamp.

**Design read.** The purpose born in the MMO gets its own family: one ledger, eleven pens, and the druid's entries are photocopies of the warrior's and rogue's.
