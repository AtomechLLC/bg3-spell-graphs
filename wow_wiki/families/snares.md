**Shared skeleton.** Soft control on one pair of auras: the roots (Entangling Roots / Frost Nova / Nature's Grasp — `Mod Root`) pin in place; the snares (Hamstring / Wing Clip / Concussive Shot / Chilled / Cone of Cold / Blast Wave — `Mod Decrease Speed`) slow the chase.

**What varies.** Root vs. snare, single vs. area, and whether damage rides along.

**Design read.** The [[families/cc|Crowd Control Cabinet]]'s outpatient wing: agency reduced, not removed.
