**Shared skeleton.** 'Summons an X under the command of the caster' — every class's companion runs the same contract. The Warlock's four demons are one contract with four signatories (0.85+); the Hunter's stable is the same system with a taming step (Tame Beast / Call Pet / Dismiss Pet / Revive Pet mirror the demon workflow verb for verb); the mounts are the same ladder twice, holy and fel; and the engine stretches to scouting eyes (Eye of Kilrogg, Eyes of the Beast), player-summoning taxi rituals, and one-shot infernals.

**What varies.** The summoned entity (demon, beast, steed, eye, party member, infernal), its permanence, the acquisition step, and the ritual cost. (The pets' own abilities — Spell Lock, Devour Magic — are taught to the pet, outside the trainer-book population studied here.)

**Design read.** Cross-class summoning is one engine wearing class-flavored leashes — BG3's container spells, twenty years early.
