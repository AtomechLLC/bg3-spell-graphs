**Shared skeleton.** 'Summons an X under the command of the caster.' The four demons are one contract with four signatories (0.85+); the mounts are the same ladder twice — Paladin and Warlock each get a level-40 horse and a level-60 upgrade, reskinned holy/fel. The engine keeps stretching: Eye of Kilrogg conjures a scouting entity, Ritual of Summoning conjures a *player*, and Inferno / Ritual of Doom conjure one-shot greater demons.

**What varies.** The summoned entity (demon, steed, eye, party member, infernal), its permanence, and the ritual cost.

**Design read.** Even the class-defining fantasy (the Warlock's demons) is a menu on one summoning engine — BG3's container spells, twenty years early, and the same contract covers scouting eyes and taxi rituals.
