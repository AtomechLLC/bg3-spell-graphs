**Shared skeleton.** 'Summons an X under the command of the caster.' The four demons are one contract with four signatories (0.85+); the mounts are the same ladder twice — Paladin and Warlock each get a level-40 horse and a level-60 upgrade, reskinned holy/fel.

**What varies.** The creature, and for mounts the speed tier.

**Design read.** Even the class-defining fantasy (the Warlock's demons) is a menu on one summoning engine — BG3's container spells, twenty years early.
