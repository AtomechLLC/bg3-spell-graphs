**Shared skeleton.** One protection effect in four grammars. The armors — 'Increases armor by X' plus a class rider — where Frost→Ice Armor is a renamed rank jump (0.9) and Demon Skin→Demon Armor the same trick in the Warlock book, with Inner Fire and Barkskin as further skins. The absorbs — the school wards (0.9+ across Fire/Frost/Shadow Ward, the WoW twin of BG3's triple Shield SKUs), Power Word: Shield, Ice Barrier, and Mana Shield, all `School Absorb`. The avoidances — Shield Block and Evasion, the same dodge/block-percent dial in two books. And the panic buttons — Ice Block / Shield Wall / Divine Shield / Divine Protection / Blessing of Protection / Fear Ward: temporary immunity or heavy mitigation on a long cooldown, one design in five class colors.

**What varies.** The refusal mechanism (armor value, absorb pool, avoidance percent, immunity window), the duration-versus-cooldown ratio, and which class's book carries it.

**Design read.** Merged on the effect, not the name: everything whose only job is 'take less damage' is one defensive design wearing four vocabularies across seven classes.
