**Shared skeleton.** 'Summons an X Totem with 5 health at the feet of the caster for Y sec, that does Z.' Twenty-one spells share the summoning chassis; the pairs inside (resistance totems, cleansing totems, stat totems) measure 0.8–0.95.

**What varies.** The totem's payload and element bucket (one totem per element active).

**Design read.** The largest single-class template family in classic — a foundry casting one mold with twenty-one fillings.
