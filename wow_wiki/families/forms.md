**Shared skeleton.** Every druid form is the identical two-aura package: `Shapeshift (X)` plus `Immunity - Mechanic (Polymorphed)`. The blended similarity clusters all of them above 0.78 — one costume engine, six costumes.

**What varies.** The form, its stat payload, and which kit it unlocks.

**Design read.** The chassis that makes [[families/cat-is-rogue|the Costume Shop]] possible — the forms are one design; the abilities they unlock are the photocopies.
