**Shared skeleton.** 'Increases armor by X…' plus a class-flavored rider. Frost→Ice Armor is a renamed rank jump (0.9); Demon Skin→Demon Armor the same trick in the Warlock book.

**What varies.** The rider (chill attackers, mana regen, health regen) and the level bracket.

**Design read.** Vertical laddering by renaming: when a rank upgrade gets a new name, the spellbook looks deeper than the design is.
