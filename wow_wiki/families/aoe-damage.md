**Shared skeleton.** Damage painted over an area: the channeled rains (Blizzard / Rain of Fire / Hurricane / Volley — a four-class verbatim clone of one channel-the-sky design), the ground fields (Flamestrike / Consecration / Hellfire), and the point-blank bursts (Arcane Explosion / Holy Nova).

**What varies.** School, shape (rain, field, nova), channel vs. instant, and whether ticks or a burst deliver it.

**Design read.** AoE damage as one product in three shapes — the rains alone are as tight a cross-class clone as the [[families/heals|heals]].
