**Shared skeleton.** 'Gives X to all party members within 30 yards.' The three resistance auras are word-identical but for the school (0.9+); the rest swap the granted stat.

**What varies.** The aura payload; one is active at a time — a modal dial like the Hunter's [[families/aspects|Aspects]].

**Design read.** The 'exclusive modal buff' engine — one slot, N interchangeable fillings — appears in four classes wearing four names: Auras, Aspects, Stances, Presences-to-be.
