**Shared skeleton.** Roll a dice pool; that many hit points of creatures are affected, in ascending order of current hit points, subtracting each affected creature from the pool; +2 dice per higher slot. Masked similarity 0.764.

**What varies.** The pool (5d8 vs. 6d10), the condition inflicted (unconscious vs. blinded), and duration (1 minute vs. 1 round).

**Design read.** The SRD's most unusual shared engine — no save, no attack roll, a bidding system against current hit points — implemented exactly twice, both at level 1.
