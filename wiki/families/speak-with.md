**Shared skeleton.** For the duration, comprehend and verbally communicate with a category of normally-mute interlocutor, whose knowledge is limited by its nature.

**What varies.** The partner (beasts / plants / corpses) and bolted-on specifics — Speak with Dead is metered at five questions; Speak with Plants adds terrain manipulation.

**Design read.** A design-slot series: the texts diverge, but the slot ('unlock dialogue with X') is the same product on three shelves.
