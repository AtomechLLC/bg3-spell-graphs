**Shared skeleton.** Creature(s) regain XdY + spellcasting modifier hit points; +1 die per higher slot; no effect on undead or constructs. Cure Wounds vs. Healing Word masks to 0.878, Healing Word vs. Prayer of Healing to 0.895.

**What varies.** Delivery parameters only: d8 touch action (Cure Wounds), d4 ranged bonus action (Healing Word), and the six-target versions of each one to four levels up. Related fixed-amount tier: Heal (70 hp) and Mass Heal (700 hp shared), plus the damage mirror Inflict Wounds.

**Design read.** One healing engine sold five times, priced along two axes — action economy and target count. This is the cleanest example of a parameterized spell line in the SRD.
