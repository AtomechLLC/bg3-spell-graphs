**Shared skeleton.** Summon from the identical CR menu — one CR 2, or two CR 1, or four CR 1/2, or eight CR 1/4; they obey verbal commands, act on their own initiative, and doubling counts on upcast. Masked similarity runs 0.93–0.97 across the trio.

**What varies.** Only the creature type filling the menu (beasts / elementals / fey) and the base level (3 / 4 / 4).

**Design read.** A literal template: the summoning engine is one block of rules text with a creature-type parameter. Druid gets all three menus.
