**Shared skeleton.** Name or describe a thing familiar to you; sense the direction to the nearest one within 1,000 feet; the text of Locate Creature tracks Locate Object clause for clause.

**What varies.** Object vs. creature vs. kind-of-beast/plant, and the counter-measure (lead blocks objects, running water blocks creatures). Locate Animals or Plants swaps the engine to a single ritual ping.

**Design read.** Notable for the flavored counter-measures: the one clearly hand-written clause in an otherwise copied text.
