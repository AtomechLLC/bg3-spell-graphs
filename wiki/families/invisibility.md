**Shared skeleton.** A creature you touch becomes invisible; anything worn or carried is invisible with it. Concentration on both.

**What varies.** Greater deletes one sentence (the ends-if-you-attack clause), shrinks duration from 1 hour to 1 minute, and costs two more levels.

**Design read.** Rare case where the upgrade is defined by removing text: the ladder rung is priced at exactly one sentence.
