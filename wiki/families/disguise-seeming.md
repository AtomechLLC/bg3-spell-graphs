**Shared skeleton.** An illusory makeover — change appearance, clothing, and gear within your rough size; physical inspection reveals it; Investigation check against your spell DC discerns it. Masked similarity 0.686.

**What varies.** Self-only 1 hour at 1st level becomes any number of creatures in range for 8 hours at 5th.
