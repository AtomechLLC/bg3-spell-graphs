**Shared skeleton.** Summon a single powerful entity to fight for you: named type up to a CR cap, friendly, obeys commands, acts on its own initiative. Elemental vs. Fey masks to 0.814; Planar Ally is the same slot brokered through your deity.

**What varies.** Creature type, CR cap, level (5–7), and the price of control — concentration with a hostile-escape clause (elemental), concentration with a polite exit (fey), or no concentration but a negotiated payment in gold (Planar Ally).

**Design read.** Sibling of [[families/conjure-table|Conjure by Menu]]: the same summoning contract at single-champion scale, with the danger-and-payment clause as the flavor differentiator.
