**Shared skeleton.** Spells of 3rd level or lower end automatically; for anything higher, make a spellcasting ability check against DC 10 + the spell's level; upcast raises the automatic threshold. Masked similarity 0.600.

**What varies.** Timing and target only: Dispel Magic is an action against a standing effect, Counterspell a reaction against one being cast.

**Design read.** One resolution subsystem serving two tactical niches — the same numbers moved to a different point in the timing chart.
