**Shared skeleton.** Transform a creature into a new form; the target takes the form's hit points; dropping to 0 in the new form reverts it to its own hit points; gear melds or drops. Animal Shapes vs. Polymorph masks to 0.710.

**What varies.** Polymorph is beast-forms only; True Polymorph adds creature-to-creature and object conversions and can become permanent; Animal Shapes is the whole-party broadcast version.

**Design read.** One transformation rulebook shared by three spells; the ladder sells scope (what forms) and persistence (how long) rather than bigger dice.
